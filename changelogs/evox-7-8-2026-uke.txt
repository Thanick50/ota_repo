# Changelog

Aug 7, 2026

-Fixed Focus Pen and Focus Pen Pro unresponsiveness after sleep mode by properly initializing via a Bluetooth request.

-Added pen remapping capabilities (e.g., emulate as S-Pen, or emulate Focus Pen Pro as a standard Focus Pen).

-Optimized dynamic refresh rate handling on pen attach/detach (automatically switches to 120Hz when detached, restores 144Hz when attached, and respects adaptive refresh rate settings).
